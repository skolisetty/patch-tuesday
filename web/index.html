    MISSION BRIEFING / PATCH TUESDAY

    Protect the network.

    You are the Blue team: 2 players + 3 AI defenders. Two hidden Red AI attackers try to break into these 20 machines. After 5 minutes the higher score wins.

    01Patch the weak spots.Fix high-risk cards before an attack.
    02Recover what falls.Restore red servers to stop their income.
    03Stay one step ahead.New weeks bring new vulnerabilities.

    How points move

    Red holds a breached machine+ its value (1-3) every tick
    Red's first breach of a machine+5
    Blue patches a card before Red tries it+ machine value x danger
    Blue restores a breached machine+3, and stops Red's income

    Live matches use real CVE records; demo mode uses sample cards. Danger is the card's risk score. Danger scores this week come from: the EPSS baseline.

    The gate: each week the pipeline tests our model against the EPSS baseline on bugs that really became exploited. Only a strict win lets our model set the danger; a tie keeps the EPSS baseline.

    Advance week: replays the next real week of vulnerability data through the Bruin pipeline. New cards drop and the attackers go after them.

    Keys

    P1P2When to use
    MoveWASD↑←↓→ Walk to the next machine. One step per tick.
    PatchEEnter Fix the top card on your machine (2 ticks). Do the high-danger cards on high-value machines first.
    RestoreR/ On a red (breached) machine: take it back (3 ticks). The most urgent move in the game.
    MonitorQR-Shift Watch your machine for a few ticks: halves the breach chance there and can reveal an attacker.
    IsolateF. Cut your machine off for 5 ticks: attackers are thrown out, but it costs 1 point per tick.

    Each player panel under the board tells you the next best move.
